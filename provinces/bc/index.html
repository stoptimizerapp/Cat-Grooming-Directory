Booking checklist

  • Confirm the groomer currently accepts cats, not only dogs.
  • Ask about matting, nail trims, bath/dry methods and handling style.
  • For mobile grooming, confirm travel coverage and setup needs.
  • Review cancellation, vaccination and temperament policies.
