Before you book a cat groomer

Ask whether the groomer regularly handles cats, which services are available for matted coats, whether sedated grooming requires a veterinarian, and how they reduce stress during drop-off, drying and nail trims.

Cat grooming directory FAQ

How do I find cat groomers near me?

Search by city, province or postal-code area, then compare cat-specific service signals, mobile availability, booking links, ratings, weekend hours and contact details.

Does every pet groomer groom cats?

No. Cats need different handling, coat knowledge and stress management. Always confirm that the business currently accepts cats before booking.

What makes this directory useful?

Each listing includes a professional summary, local signals, mobile indicators, source links, booking details and practical questions to ask before an appointment.

Directory details are compiled from public business listings and provider websites. Always confirm services, prices, hours, vaccination requirements and appointment policies directly with the grooming business before booking.
